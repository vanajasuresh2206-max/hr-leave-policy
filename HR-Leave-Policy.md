# HR Leave Policy

## 1. Casual Leave

Employees are eligible for 12 days of casual leave per calendar year.

Casual leave can normally be taken for personal requirements.

## 2. Sick Leave

Employees are eligible for 12 days of sick leave per calendar year.

Sick leave may be taken for illness, medical appointments,
or health-related reasons.

A medical certificate may be requested for sick leave
of more than 3 consecutive working days.

## 3. Annual Leave

Employees are eligible for 18 days of annual leave per year.

Annual leave should normally be requested at least 3 working
days before the planned leave.

## 4. Manager Approval

Manager approval is required for:

- Annual leave
- More than 2 consecutive days of casual leave
- More than 3 consecutive days of sick leave
- Any leave that conflicts with critical project requirements

## 5. Leave Balance

An employee cannot take leave if the requested number of days
exceeds the available leave balance.

## 6. Weekends

Saturday and Sunday are not counted as leave days unless
the employee's work schedule specifically includes weekends.

## 7. Important Rule

The HR leave policy is the source of truth.

If the policy does not contain enough information to make a decision,
the system must respond:

"Insufficient policy information. HR/Manager review required."

The system must not invent HR rules.
